# Recorded firearm sounds

AR-15, AK-47, Walther PPQ and Benelli Nova recordings from The Free Firearm Sound Library by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras and Matthew Nanney. CC0 1.0.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
Mirror: https://github.com/buddingmonkey/FreeFirearmsSFXLibrary/tree/beb2f4041f3d6740fa0aeaf0e71159bd65a78c1b
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Prepared from lossless source WAVs. Edited onset, mono 48kHz conversion, level adjustment and boundary fades. No synthetic gunshot layers. Weapon models and audio use generic gameplay categories; the AK mesh is an AK-74 and its report comes from an AK-47 recording. Existing reload cues use recorded AR-15 mechanisms.
