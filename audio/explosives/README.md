# Real explosion recording candidates

Two lossless WAV recordings of an agricultural bird-scarer cannon by Joseph SARDIN, downloaded from BigSoundBank under CC0. These are genuine explosive report recordings, not recordings of C4 or a military hand grenade. Sources are retained byte-for-byte in sources/. Game WAVs are mono 48kHz/16-bit conversions; no synthesised layers or added reverb. Close recording was made around 10m; mid around 150m. Audition and balance in-game before treating the sound as final premium quality. Metadata and source links are in SOURCES.json.
