# Recorded sky and landing audio

Enabled in gameplay. Helicopter #1 by DenisChardonnet and Strong Wind #1 by Joseph SARDIN, CC0, from BigSoundBank. Original lossless WAVs and hashes are preserved in real-sources/. Sources: https://bigsoundbank.com/helicopter-1-s0263.html and https://bigsoundbank.com/strong-wind-1-s0146.html . These are a cabin helicopter recording and outdoor tree wind, adapted for the prototype; they are not actual skydiving recordings.

The rotor and wind loops use mono 48kHz conversion, linear level adjustment, a 400ms wrap crossfade and Vorbis encoding. Canopy loop uses recorded Fabric Flapping by IENBA plus a quiet layer of the real wind. Deployment uses recorded canvas snap and cloth; boots use recorded mountain-boot landings. Those existing foley sources came from public HQ MP3 previews, not original lossless masters. Individual author, CC0 licence and source URLs are preserved in sources-and-credits.json. No synthesised wind remains in the active loops. BUILD.json records loop durations and levels. Subjective balancing still needs physical-device listening.
