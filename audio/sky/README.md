# Industrial Yard — Sky Audio v1

First sound-design preview pack, prepared 5 November 2026. No game files changed.

## Listen first

Play sky_drop_preview.mp3 (26 seconds):
- 0–6 seconds: helicopter rotor.
- 5.5–14 seconds: leaving the helicopter and increasing freefall wind.
- 14 seconds: parachute deployment snap.
- 14.4–23.6 seconds: quieter canopy glide and fabric movement.
- 23.3 seconds: boot touchdown.

The sequence is an example mix; timing will follow actual player actions in the game.

## Included sounds

- helicopter_rotor_loop — helicopter rotor bed.
- freefall_wind_loop — designed/synthesised rushing wind.
- parachute_canopy_loop — cloth movement with quiet air underneath.
- parachute_deploy — canvas snap and settling cloth tail.
- landing_boots_01 / 02 / 03 — three boot-impact variations on rock; audition against the yard surface.

All individual WAV files: mono, 48 kHz, 16-bit PCM. Three ambient loops also supplied as OGG Vorbis. Enable looping only for files ending in _loop. Use WAV for short one-shots. Mono files can be placed spatially when needed; the local player's freefall/canopy beds should follow that player.

## Quality and source status

This pack uses publicly accessible HQ MP3 previews from Freesound, NOT the original lossless recordings. Converting them to WAV does not restore original quality. Treat this as a first audition pack; full-quality originals can replace the sources through normal Freesound account downloads.

DC removal, filtering, level balancing, gentle compression, one-shot fades and loop crossfades have been applied. Automated checks cover file integrity, clipping, duration and loop-boundary sample jumps. Subjective listening and in-game testing are still pending; these checks do not prove the sounds feel right in play.

The canopy and deployment effects are designed from cloth foley, not real parachute recordings. No voices or soundtrack are included.
