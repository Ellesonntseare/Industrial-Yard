# Armory alarm

`fire_alarm_bell.wav` derives from **fire alarm bell** by **msx2plus**, published 11 March 2023 as a field recording on Freesound.

- Source and declared license: https://freesound.org/people/msx2plus/sounds/678345/
- License: CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Downloaded public high-quality MP3 preview: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/678/678345_14784311-hq.mp3
- Changes: converted to mono, 22,050 Hz, 16-bit PCM WAV for positional playback. No synthetic audio added.

The original recording's stereo FLAC is 96 kHz. This game's source is the publicly downloadable preview, converted as described above.
