# Industrial Yard — Real Firearms v1

Prepared 5 November 2026. Free real-firearm recording selection. No synthetic or AI-generated gunshot layers, added oscillators, artificial distant-shot filtering, pitch randomisation or added reverb were used. These are recorded weapon takes and recorded weapon mechanisms, with trimming, mono conversion, sample-rate conversion, linear level adjustment and short boundary fades. Editing a recorded sound is still required for gameplay timing.

## Listen

real_firearms_preview.mp3 is a 38-second listening comparison, not a gameplay soundtrack:
- 0.5 and 4 sec: two different AR-15 close takes.
- 7.5 sec: real AR-15 empty trigger.
- 8.4 sec: recorded magazine/bolt actions assembled as an example reload.
- 11.7 sec: AR-15 after reload.
- 15 and 18 sec: actual mid-distance AR-15 recordings.
- 21 sec: AK-47 single shot; 24.2 sec: a recorded short burst.
- 28 and 30.2 sec: two Walther PPQ pistol takes.
- 33.2 sec: Benelli Nova shotgun.

The accompanying WAV preview is lossless. MP3 is supplied only for convenient listening. Prepared game-ready WAVs were made directly from lossless WAV sources, not MP3 previews.

## Included

26 individual game-ready WAVs: 16 gunfire takes/recorded bursts, eight AR-15/AK-47 mechanism cues, and two example reload assemblies. Each game-ready file is mono 48 kHz/16-bit PCM. Do not loop one-shots. In particular, the recorded burst files contain multiple rounds and must not be triggered once per bullet. Use single-shot variants for variable firing rates.

Actual near/mid perspective comes from the library's recording index; distance in metres is not established. Apply runtime spatial positioning and attenuation for the opponent, and use the local shooter's mix separately. Never play near and mid clips together at full level by default. Natural outdoor tails remain in the recordings; interior reverb needs in-game testing.

The original-recordings folder preserves all 15 downloaded WAVs byte-for-byte. Prepared shot recordings are 24-bit/96 kHz stereo; consult source-manifest.json for each raw mechanism file's format. The two reload examples are assemblies for audition; the gameplay animation should trigger magazine and bolt cues individually. Some action excerpts may need final timing adjustments after listening against the animation.

Gun names identify audio options, not a decision to add those guns to the game. No gameplay files were modified. This set does not include impact/casing effects: those need original-recording selections separately.
